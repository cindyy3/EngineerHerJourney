Our Mission

Despite making up nearly half the workforce, women remain underrepresented in engineering. Many girls grow up without role models in the field or exposure to the vast possibilities within engineering. EngineerHer Journey was created to change that—to ignite curiosity, break barriers, and empower the next generation of women in engineering.
